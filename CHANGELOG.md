# Changelog

## Buff Tracker 1.0.0 — 2026-11-12

First public release, for Disfigure with BepInEx 6.0.0-be.785 (Unity IL2CPP, win-x64).

- Shield Barrier and Projectile Barrier: time left until each shield comes back after it
  breaks.
- Shield Flare: kills with the Shield Barrier active, out of 100, while it is unlocked and not
  active yet.
- Kill Spree: current bonus and seconds left before it resets.
- Rapid Fire, Ricochet Rebound and Magnetic Rounds: current bonus.
- Each indicator shows the icon of its card, at the top right of the screen, under the
  score.
- F7 opens an options panel to turn each indicator on or off (keys 1 to 9 also work while
  it is open). The choice is saved in
  `BepInEx\config\dalessandro.disfigure.bufftracker.cfg`.
